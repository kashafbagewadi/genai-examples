## Comparison with AI
A Python-based AI module that compares chipset used between two major iPhone models.

### Description
Constructs a parameterized prompt asking the AI to compare the two given iPhone models (item1 and item2) in terms of only the chip used, ensuring the response is given in one word only.
e.g. for models iphone 13 and iphone 17 -> A15-Bionic-vs-A19
